// faq

Questions, answered

Do I need to know anything technical?

Not at all. We explain everything in plain English and handle the technical side. After launch we give you a short walkthrough so you're comfortable making simple updates yourself.

How long does a website take?

A small site of up to 5 pages usually takes 2–4 weeks, depending mostly on how quickly content and feedback come together. Site jobs and admin tasks are often done within a week.

Who owns the website and domain?

You do. Your domain, hosting accounts and content are registered in your name. If you ever decide to move on, you take everything with you.

What is a business email on my domain?

Instead of yourbusiness@gmail.com, you get a professional address like you@yourbusiness.com. We set it up with a provider such as Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 and configure it so your emails don't land in spam.

What is HTTPS and why do I need it?

HTTPS (the padlock in your browser) encrypts traffic between your site and your visitors. Without it, browsers show a “Not secure” warning and search engines rank you lower. We install the certificate and make sure it renews automatically.

What kinds of tasks can you automate?

Anything repetitive: sending quotes, booking appointments, creating invoices and payment links, tracking stock, moving data between spreadsheets and apps, or building simple internal tools your team uses every day.

Do I have to sign up for a monthly plan?

No. Care and support plans are optional. Many clients choose one for peace of mind, but you're free to just book one-off work whenever you need it.
